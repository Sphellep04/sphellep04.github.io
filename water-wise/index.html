Water Wise

Namibia is one of the driest countries in Africa, and every drop counts. Fix the leaks and running taps before your tank runs dry.

How it works

Leaks pop up around the house and drain your tank. Tap a leaking spot to fix it. The longer a leak runs the faster it drains. Tap a passing rain cloud to top the tank up. Keyboard: number keys 1 to 9.

Choose a mode. Relaxed drains the tank slowly, Tough springs leaks faster. Each mode keeps its own best.
