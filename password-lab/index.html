    How is this worked out?

    We imagine a criminal who has stolen a database of scrambled passwords and can try 10 billion guesses a second on fast computers. That is a tough case, but it happens. Common words, names, years and keyboard patterns are tried first, so they count for very little. Length and randomness are what really protect you. The time is a rough estimate, not a promise.
