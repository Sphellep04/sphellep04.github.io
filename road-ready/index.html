  • Slow down early. Speed decides how far you need to stop and how badly a crash hurts.
  • Give yourself space. Leave a big gap, more in rain, and never overtake without a clear view.
  • Full attention. No phone, no alcohol and stop for a rest when you are tired.
  • Everyone buckles up. Seatbelts for every passenger and a proper child seat for small children.

The Motor Vehicle Accident Fund has said that around 72% of crashes in Namibia are caused by human error, so most are down to choices like these. Source: The Namibian.
