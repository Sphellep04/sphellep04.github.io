Sort It Out

Rubbish goes in the wrong bin more often than you think. Sort twenty items before you run out of lives.

How it works

An item appears. Tap the bin it belongs in before the timer runs out. You have three lives, and each wrong bin or timeout costs one. Keys 1 to 7 also work. Bin colours differ from town to town, so always read the labels.

  • Reduce first. The best waste is the waste you never make. Carry a bag and a bottle.
  • Reuse next. Jars, tubs and cartons can be used again before they are thrown away.
  • Rinse and dry. Clean containers recycle better. Food and grease spoil good paper and plastic.
  • Special items need special places. Batteries, old phones, bulbs, paint and medicine should not go in household rubbish. Ask your municipality or shops about drop-off points.
