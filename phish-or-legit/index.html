        If something goes wrong

        • Money. Call your bank or mobile money provider straight away using the number on your card or in their app.
        • SIM or phone. Contact your mobile provider from another phone and block the SIM.
        • Accounts. Change the password from a safe device and turn on two-factor authentication.
        • Report. Keep screenshots and tell the police. Reporting scams helps protect others.

        This is general awareness, not professional advice. Every bank and provider has its own process, so check with them.
