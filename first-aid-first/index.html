How it works

Each emergency gives you a set of shuffled steps. Tap them in the order you would do them. You earn up to three stars for each one. Wrong taps cost a star, and Hint shows you the next step.

This is awareness, not training. A hands-on first aid course teaches the real skills, and in an emergency call for help straight away.

    • Stay safe first. Check for danger before you help so you do not become a second casualty.
    • Call for help early. In Namibia the police number is 10111. Ambulance numbers differ from town to town, so save the one for your area.
    • Do the simple things well. Pressure on a bleed, cool water on a burn and hard, fast chest compressions really do save lives.
    • Learn it properly. Ask a recognised provider about a first aid course. It is a skill you never regret having.
